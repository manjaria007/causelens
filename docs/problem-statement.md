# Problem Statement

Business teams can see that a KPI changed, but identifying why it changed is
often a slow, manual process involving segmentation, exploratory analysis,
statistical testing, release/event review, and experimentation.

CauseLens aims to shorten this investigation while preserving analytical
rigor. It should distinguish correlation from causation, quantify uncertainty,
and recommend a follow-up experiment when the available evidence is
insufficient.

## Target users

- Product managers
- Growth and experimentation teams
- Business analysts
- Data scientists
- Analytics teams

## Example question

> Why did checkout conversion fall from 8.2% to 6.9%?

The intended outcome is not merely a narrative explanation. CauseLens should
produce an evidence-backed investigation with reproducible analytical steps,
uncertainty, assumptions, and a recommended next action.
